Anthropogenic (man-made) Features on the Moon

Coordinates of anthropogenic (man-made) objects on the Moon as measured by Wagner, et al. (2017) using Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) NAC images combined with Earth-based laser-ranging measurements. These objects include soft-landed spacecraft, rovers, deployed scientific payloads, and spacecraft impact craters. More recent anthropogenic objects have also been included. Some objects are excluded if we have not yet located them in LROC NAC imagery. Coordinates for retroreflectors are from laser ranging; all other coordinates were calculated as described in Wagner et al. (2017), or recorded from DTM orthophotos where that resulted in lower uncertainty. The method used to derive coordinates can be found in the COORD_SRC column. For more information, refer to: Wagner, R. V., Nelson, D. M., Plescia, J. B., Robinson, M. S., Speyerer, E. J., & Mazarico, E. (2017). Coordinates of anthropogenic features on the Moon. Icarus, 283, 92-103. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2016.05.011

When citing this product, use the following reference: Wagner, R. V., Nelson, D. M., Plescia, J. B., Robinson, M. S., Speyerer, E. J., & Mazarico, E. (2017). Coordinates of anthropogenic features on the Moon. Icarus, 283, 92-103. doi:10.1016/j.icarus.2016.05.011

Point shapefiles are available in the IAU Moon 2000 Geographic Coordinate System in both the -180 to 180 and 0 to 360 longitude domains.

Shapefile Field Descriptions:


OBJECT_ID: A highly abbreviated name indicating the mission and object with no spaces or special characters
SHORT_NAME: A short, plain-language title for the object
VEHCL_NAME: Full name of the vehicle as used by its creators, where applicable. May not be unique.
MISSION: The mission associated with the feature
OBJECT: The type of man-made feature/object
LATITUDE: Latitude in degrees
LON_360: Longitude in degrees from 0° to 360°
LONGITUDE: Longitude in degrees from -180° to 180°
RADIUS: Radius of the feature in meters
RAD_SRC: Source of the radius information
COORD_SRC: Source of the latitude/longitude information
UNCERTAIN: Uncertainty of the coordinates in meters
DATE: Date in UTC that the object arrived at the surface of the Moon
REFERENCES: One or more URLs (separated by "; ") for papers or websites that support the identification. All publicly-accessible websites are also archived on Archive.org.

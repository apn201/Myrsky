## What judges see, in order

1. Project page text (FORM.md): summary, then a "Try it in two minutes" box at the top of Project Details, then screenshots between paragraphs.
2. Demo: 30 s video, or the 7-slide PDF.
3. The live site, the info page ("There is no camera") and the code.

The rubric is Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation. The text answers each: students visiting abandoned hardware (impact, relevance), a fake 1997 webcam that turns out to be a calculation (creativity), the 5.15° vs 5.1° and 272° vs 273° cross-checks plus the real / modelled / invented table (validity), screenshots and video (presentation).

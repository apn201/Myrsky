# 30-second demo video: script and shot list

Hard limit: **30 seconds or less** (over 30 s = not eligible for Global judging). Aim for 28 s.
English voice-over or English captions. Captions are in `captions.srt` (burn them in: judges often watch muted).
No people under 18, no music you don't have rights to, no NASA logo. A silent video with captions is fine.

## Tools (all free on Windows 11)

- Record: **Clipchamp** (built in) → "Record & create" → Screen, or **OBS Studio**, or Xbox Game Bar (Win+Alt+R).
- Browser at 1920×1080, zoom 100 %. Use the page's **fullscreen** button for the picture-only shots.
- Edit in Clipchamp: trim, speed up the REW clip, add the title and end cards (text on black), import `captions.srt` or type captions, export 1080p.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** or Public. Test the link in a private window before pasting it.
